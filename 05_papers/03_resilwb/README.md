# 03 — The cost of resilience

**Full title:** The cost of resilience: Life satisfaction among disadvantaged high achievers in PISA 2025.

**Companion report:** The price of progress: Achievement, school progression and adolescent life satisfaction in PISA 2025.

This active wellbeing project is being developed in a separate session. It examines life satisfaction among disadvantaged high achievers, the role of socio-emotional skills, and school progression. The 2 reports belong to the same project.

## Reports and files

- `03_manuscript/reportA_resilience.qmd`: The cost of resilience.
- `03_manuscript/reportB_progress.qmd`: The price of progress.
- `01_code/`: estimation, sensitivity checks, and report helpers; see its README for the current analysis steps.
- `02_results/`: reserved for approved result snapshots.
- `04_output/`: local estimates, figures, and logs.
- `05_archive/` and `06_submissions/`: local historical and submission artifacts.

The report sources are under active development; ongoing session edits are preserved locally and are committed by that session. PDFs and data remain Git-ignored.
